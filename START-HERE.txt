Sticky Business Access 0.8.2 — simplified player package, revision 1

1. Extract the entire ZIP with Windows Extract All.
2. Close Sticky Business. Run Setup.bat to install or update.
3. Start your supported screen reader, then launch the game through Steam.

The support folder must stay beside Setup.bat. You do not need to open it
or copy its files yourself. Initial loader preparation may take a few minutes
and needs an internet connection.

Tab / Shift+Tab switches sections in Creative Corner, sheet design, packing and shop screens.
Arrows browse within a section. F6 categories, F7 items, F9 placed items.
In upgrades: F6 categories, F7 items, F9 controls. In Customize: F7 colours, F9 backgrounds.
Enter or Tab finishes positioning and returns to the same available choice.
F1 gives contextual help. F2 repeats. F5 opens the information view.

To remove the mod, close the game and run Uninstall.bat.
Full controls and installation information: support/README.md.
BepInEx help: support/docs/BEPINEX-TROUBLESHOOTING.md.

The Class::Init “using a substitute” warning indicates a loader fallback.
It has not prevented startup in the tested build. It remains in the log.
A separate console is unnecessary; fresh installs keep it hidden. An old
loader configuration may still enable it. See the BepInEx help document.

If the game opens without speech, fully exit Steam, reopen Steam, then launch
from its library with your supported screen reader running. Closing only Steam's window may not exit it.
Your saves and inventory behavior are preserved by this update.
